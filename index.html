Usine a Clips is a desktop tool that helps a creator turn the highlights of gaming live streams into short vertical videos: automatic subtitles, vertical framing and a ready-to-use caption.

When the creator connects their TikTok account, the tool can send a finished video to their own TikTok account as a draft. Nothing is ever posted automatically: the creator reviews every video and publishes it themselves in the TikTok app.

The tool runs entirely on the creator's own computer. It is not a public service and does not collect data from other people.
