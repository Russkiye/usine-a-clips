Privacy Policy

Usine a Clips runs on the user's own computer. Custom Palace does not operate any server that receives your data.

Data from TikTok

When you connect your TikTok account, the Tool receives, with your consent: your basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and access tokens. They are stored only on your computer and used only to show which account is connected and to send the videos you choose to your own account.

Videos

A video is sent to TikTok only when you explicitly ask for it. Videos stay on your computer otherwise.

What we never do

No selling or sharing of data with third parties, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking.

Deleting your data

Disconnect TikTok in the Tool (this deletes the stored tokens) and/or revoke access in TikTok (Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions). Deleting the Tool's folder removes everything else.
